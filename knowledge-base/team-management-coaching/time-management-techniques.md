# Productivity Cheat Sheet

**Summary:** Practical time-management techniques: Pomodoro, 3/3/3, Eisenhower Matrix, Eat the Frog, Seinfeld Strategy, Time Blocking.

## Techniques

1. Pomodoro Technique
- List your tasks.
- Set a 25-minute timer, work in focused bursts.
- Take a 5-minute break.
- Repeat and take a longer break after four sessions.

2. 3/3/3 Method (From Oliver Burkeman)
- 3 Hours: Spend 3 hours on an important project each day.
- 3 Tasks: Complete 3 shorter urgent tasks or meetings.
- 3 Maintenance Tasks: Do 3 tasks that keep life running smoothly.

3. Eisenhower Matrix
- Urgent and Important: Do it immediately.
- Important but Not Urgent: Schedule it for later.
- Urgent but Not Important: Delegate it.
- Not Urgent and Not Important: Eliminate it.

4. Eat the Frog
- Do your hardest task first.

5. Seinfeld Strategy
- Set a goal, mark a calendar, keep a streak and never miss 2 days in a row.

6. Time Blocking
- Identify tasks, group activities, assign time slots, plot blocks in a calendar and take breaks.
