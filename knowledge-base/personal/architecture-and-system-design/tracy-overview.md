# Tracy — Overview

**Summary:** Purpose, problem statement, objectives and high-level justification for a unified platform-level monitoring tool called Tracy.

## Purpose
Provide unified visibility across services (shopping, payment, backoffice) to improve incident detection, troubleshooting and platform-wide reliability.

## Problem
Teams currently monitor per-service; there is no unified view or entity-based tracing of a flight booking through the entire platform.

## Objective
Introduce a tracing/observability layer that can detect bottlenecks, provide per-transaction visibility and enable faster incident response.
