# Service Boundaries & Splitting Entities

**Summary:** Transcript exploring how to define service boundaries by analyzing entities and splitting them across contexts (e.g., Product in Catalog vs. Sales vs. Warehouse). It emphasizes that "Entity as a Service" is an anti-pattern and that different contexts should own different slices of the same conceptual entity (CodeOpinion).

**Source:** [Defining Service Boundaries by Splitting Entities](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lGvYHmvXiyk) — CodeOpinion.
