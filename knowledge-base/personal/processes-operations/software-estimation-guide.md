# Estimation

**Summary:** Practical advice on realistic software estimates; recommends padding estimates (25–50%) to protect delivery timelines and expectations.

No matter what estimate you give me as a Software Engineer, I am going to add 25–50% to it.

As a Software Engineer it can be anxiety-inducing to make sure you are estimating as realistically as possible.

Flip your narrative. Whatever your worst case scenario is, add more time. And then probably some more. If your top end estimate begins to sound a bit far fetched, you are on the right track.

I know, there is an anti-estimate mindset—this has only ever been fiction from my 15 years in the industry. You WILL get asked for estimates, more so the more senior you become.

I want to protect you, the team and the company and ensure we have a smooth delivery. No one wins from promising too much.
