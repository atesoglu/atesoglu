# Tell Me About Yourself: The Two-Minute Pitch

**Summary:** A concise, repeatable structure for a two-minute interview pitch: results, strength and one ask.

## Formula
1. Most Relevant Results: "I have been [role] for [time]... Recently I [outcome] which helped [company result]".
2. Your Biggest Strength: Describe one strength and how you apply it.
3. Your One Ask: What you want from the role and the team (a short closing question). 

## Example
"I have been in digital marketing for 10 years... Recently I managed a campaign that generated 500+ new leads... My main strength is creating content on LinkedIn... I'm looking for a team that values a get-it-done attitude."
