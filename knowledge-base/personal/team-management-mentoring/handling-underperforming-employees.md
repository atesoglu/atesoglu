# Handling Underperforming Employees: Lessons for New Managers

**Summary:** Practical approach to diagnosing and addressing underperformance: clarify expectations, provide support and hold timely conversations.

_As a new manager, handling underperforming employees is one of the toughest challenges. Dave Kline, a seasoned leader, reflected on his early mistakes and shared practical methods for addressing underperformance decisively, before it negatively impacts the team._

## The Core Problem

Underperformance is rarely just about the employee. Often, it reflects:

- Unclear expectations
- Insufficient support or resources
- Poor feedback loops
- Delayed or avoided conversations
