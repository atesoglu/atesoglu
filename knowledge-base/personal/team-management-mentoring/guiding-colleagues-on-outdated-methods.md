# Guiding Colleagues on Outdated Methods

**Summary:** Guidelines for respectfully and constructively addressing outdated methods with colleagues, emphasizing industry standards and professional growth.

If you need to issue a guideline for a colleague regarding outdated methods, approach the situation in a respectful and constructive manner.

## Approaches

- **Explain current industry standards**: Contrast the outdated method with modern practices (e.g., Agile, Lean, Scrum) and their wide adoption.
- **Explain the "Why"**: Highlight how updated methodologies lead to efficiency, productivity and better results.
- **Recommend research**: Suggest training, workshops, or reading materials to deepen understanding.
- **Offer support**: Offer to pair program or provide resources/tutorials to help the transition.
- **Frame as growth**: Emphasize that adopting new methods is an opportunity for personal and professional development.

**Goal:** Create a supportive environment where the colleague feels helped rather than criticized.
