# CTO: The First 30 Days

**Summary:** Transitioning from "Operational functionalist" to "Technological Visionary & Company Leader."

---

## 1. Days 1–10: The Horizon View
- [ ] **Board & Investor Deep Dive**: Understand the company's valuation levers and the investors' 5-year outlook.
- [ ] **The "Tech Moat" Audit**: What is our unique technological advantage? Is it defensible?
- [ ] **Customer "Vision" Tour**: Meet with top-tier clients. Don't talk about features; talk about their 3-year challenges.

## 2. Days 11–20: Identifying "Extinction Level" Risks
- [ ] **Technological obsolescence**: Is our core stack dying? What new technology (e.g. AI, Quantum, Edge) could disrupt us in 24 months?
- [ ] **Dependency Audit**: Review our reliance on third-party platforms (AWS/Azure/Google). What is our "Exit" or "Multi-cloud" reality?
- [ ] **The "Innovation" Leak**: How much of our engineering capacity is actually innovating vs. just keeping the lights on?

## 3. Days 21–30: The Technological North Star
- [ ] **Draft the 3-Year Technical Vision**: Where must the tech be to support a 10x growth in customers?
- [ ] **Establish the "Innovation Lab"**: Define how the company will experimental with high-risk, high-reward technologies.
- [ ] **The "CEO Partner" Protocol**: Align with the CEO on how technical breakthroughs will drive the next product category.

---

## 4. Key Behavioral Shift
- **Vision over Operations**: Your VP of Engineering runs the machine; you decide *which machine* the company should be building for the future.
- **External over Internal**: Your value is increasingly found in the market, with investors and with futuristic partners.
