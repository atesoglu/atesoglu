# VP of Engineering: Role Expectations

**Summary:** The head of the engineering "function." Accountable for the human capital, operational efficiency and delivery excellence of the entire engineering organization.

---

## 1. Core Accountability
The VP of Engineering ensures the company has a world-class engineering team that is cost-effective, high-performing and culturally healthy. They bridge the gap between "Technology Capabilities" and "Business Growth."

## 2. Impact Areas

### 👥 People & Culture (Primary Focus)
- **Executive Leadership**: Recruits and grows the Director and VP-level leadership bench.
- **Functional Health**: Accountable for global eNPS, turnover rates and the DEI (Diversity, Equity and Inclusion) of the engineering org.
- **Employer Brand**: Acts as the face of engineering to the outside world to attract top-tier talent.

### ⚙️ Operational Efficiency
- **COGS Management**: Accountable for the multi-million dollar infrastructure budget.
- **Global Efficiency**: Defines the universal engineering metrics (DORA, FTE efficiency) and drives improvements across the whole org.
- **Risk & Compliance**: Ensures the organization meets security standards (SOC2, ISO) and regulatory requirements.

### 💼 Business Growth
- **Delivery Strategy**: Negotiates with Product and Sales on massive multi-quarter delivery commitments.
- **M&A Readiness**: Evaluates technical and team health for potential acquisitions (or being acquired).
