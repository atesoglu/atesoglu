# Senior Engineering Manager: The First 30 Days

**Summary:** Transitioning from "Team Leader" to "Organizational System Designer."

---

## 1. Days 1–10: The Manager-of-Managers Transition
- [ ] **Skip-Level 1:1s**: Meet the individual contributors reporting to your EMs. Listen for gaps between management vision and day-to-day reality.
- [ ] **Manager 1:1s**: Understand the challenges of your direct report EMs. Are they struggling with delegation? Do they have clear goals?
- [ ] **Resource Audit**: Review the budget, headcount and hiring plans for your entire department/domain.

## 2. Days 11–20: Identifying Departmental Friction
- [ ] **Cross-Team Dependencies**: Map the most common blockers between your teams and other departments.
- [ ] **Standardization Check**: Are your teams using wildly different processes for CRs, deployments, or on-call? Identify where consistency is needed.
- [ ] **Business Alignment**: Sync with the Product Director to ensure the department-wide roadmap is technically feasible.

## 3. Days 21–30: Developing the "Manager Backbone"
- [ ] **Define Management Rituals**: Establish a "Managers Forum" or sync for your leads to share best practices.
- [ ] **Identify "High-Potentials"**: Who is the next EM or Principal in your organization? Start sponsorship plans.
- [ ] **Define Department Metrics**: Move beyond team-level DORA metrics to aggregate health and throughput indicators for the whole domain.

---

## 4. Key Behavioral Shift
- **Lead Through Leads**: Your primary output is now the *effectiveness of your managers*. Resist the urge to fix team-level problems directly.
- **Focus on the "Whitespaces"**: Spend your time on the problems that fall between teams (inter-team dependencies).
