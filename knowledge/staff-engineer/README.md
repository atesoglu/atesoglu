# Staff Engineer

Curated resources, frameworks, and reflections specific to the Staff/Principal Engineer track.

## Scope

This area focuses on the **unique challenges of Staff+ roles**:

- Scope expansion beyond a single team
- Technical strategy and organizational influence
- Cross-team alignment and architecture governance
- Mentoring senior engineers and engineering managers
- Balancing hands-on work with strategic leadership
