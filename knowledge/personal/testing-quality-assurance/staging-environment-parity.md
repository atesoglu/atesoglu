# Staging Environment Parity

**Summary:** Principles for keeping staging close to production. Detailed management guidance lives in [Staging Environment Management and Optimization](staging-environment-management.md).

## Core Principle

Staging should be close enough to production that configuration drift, deployment differences, observability gaps, and capacity assumptions are exposed before release.

For assessment steps, metrics, and improvement planning, use the canonical [staging management guide](staging-environment-management.md).
