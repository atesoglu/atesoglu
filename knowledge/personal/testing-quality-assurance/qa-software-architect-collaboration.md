# QA and Software Architect Collaboration

**Summary:** Best practices for ensuring effective collaboration between software architects and testers to reduce defects and improve quality.

Software architects and testers should work closely together in an enterprise-level project. This collaboration ensures that the architecture is thoroughly tested and issues are addressed early.

## Best Practices for Collaboration

1.  **Clearly defined roles**: Ensure both parties understand their responsibilities and how they complement each other.
2.  **Open Communication**: Maintain regular dialogue to identify issues early.
3.  **Integration of testing**: Treat testing as part of the development process, not a separate phase.
4.  **Joint planning**: Work together to plan testing strategies and identify risks.
5.  **Shared goals**: Align on the common objective of delivering high-quality software.

By following these steps, teams can reduce the risk of defects and deliver higher quality products.
