# 7 Big Mistakes I Made as a New Manager

**Summary:** Lessons and practical tips for new managers to avoid common leadership mistakes.

1. Assuming silence meant agreement
- The Mistake: Interpreting silence as agreement from team members, especially across cultures.
- Tip: Proactively seek input and provide multiple ways to give feedback.

2. Underestimating the impact of small wins
- The Mistake: Overlooking small accomplishments that motivate teams.
- Tip: Recognize small victories and celebrate progress.

3. Waiting too long to address performance issues
- The Mistake: Hoping problems go away without timely conversations.
- Tip: Address issues promptly with empathy and clarity.

4. Not setting clear expectations
- The Mistake: Assuming the team understands unspoken expectations.
- Tip: Set clear goals and success criteria for projects.

5. Not managing up
- The Mistake: Failing to regularly update stakeholders and ask for support.
- Tip: Share progress and advocate for your team's needs.

6. Trying to solve every problem myself
- The Mistake: Micromanaging and not empowering others.
- Tip: Delegate and coach to help team members grow.

7. Not setting boundaries for myself
- The Mistake: Being available 24/7 and burning out.
- Tip: Protect your energy and role-model healthy habits.
