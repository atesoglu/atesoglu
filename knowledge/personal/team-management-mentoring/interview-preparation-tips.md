# Interview Preparation Tips

**Summary:** Practical guidance and sample answers for common interview questions to help candidates articulate experience and fit.

**1. Can you tell me a little about yourself?**

To answer, walk them through your background, starting with how you began your career or your current line of work. Highlight key accomplishments, career moves and end by sharing what you're looking to do next and why you're job hunting.

**Good answer sample:**

"I started my career in Marketing after graduating with a Business degree in 2011. I've spent my entire career at Google, receiving 3 promotions and 4 awards for outstanding performance. I'm looking to join a smaller company now and take on more leadership and project management."

---

**2. How did you hear about the position?**

This is one of the simplest questions in any interview, but answering incorrectly can ruin your chances at the job.

**Good answer samples:**

"I saw the job posted on a website and the position seemed interesting so I wanted to learn more."
