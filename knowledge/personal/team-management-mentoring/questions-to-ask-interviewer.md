# Questions to Ask Interviewer in the Meeting

**Summary:** A categorized checklist of questions to learn about company, role, team and expectations during interviews.

## COMPANY

- **What do you/employees like most about working here?**
- **How would you describe the corporate culture here? What are the company's goals for the future?**
- **What about the overall style of management at the company? What kind of leadership/management style do you promote?**
- **What, if any, are your plans for transitioning from remote to in-person work?**
- **How does the company take an idea from inception to completion?**
- **How about the innovation process? Is it "move fast and break things" or precision and artistry?**
- **What would you say is the time spent on creating new products/projects?**
- **What model of reinforcement do you use to correct and instruct? How is feedback given to employees?**

## PEOPLE

- **How many people have joined the company recently?**
- **How many people have left the company in the last year?**
- **Are there opportunities for professional development? If so, what do those look like?**

## TEAM

- **Who will I be working most closely with? Could you tell me a little bit about the person I would report to directly?**
- **What kind of processes and technology do you use to work collaboratively?**
- **May I ask why the last person left the job?**

## ROLE

- **What are the primary responsibilities of the position? What do you see as the most challenging aspect of this job?**
- **What do the day-to-day responsibilities of the role look like?**
- **What does success look like in this position and how do you measure it?**
- **What would I be expected to accomplish in my first month/year on the job?**
- **Do you expect the responsibilities for this role to change in the near future?**
- **How does senior management view/interact with the person in this position?**
- **How does the position fit in relation to the rest of the organization?**
- **Is there a career path that someone in this position would be expected to follow?**
- **How well do people with my background adjust to working here?**
- **Could you tell me what it looks like to get a performance review?**
- **Are there any special projects that I would work on soon?**
- **How is information documented and shared across projects and departments?**
- **What type of things would I need to achieve to advance within the position/company?**
- **What are the current focus areas for the company as a whole?**
- **How do you reward employees for good work?**
- **Do you see the role expanding in the future?**
- **Do you have any on-the-job training?**

## ADDITIONAL QUESTIONS

- **Big Hairy Audacious Goal (BHAG) seems authentic and it seems the company has already drawn the layout for the future.**
- **How about the rivalry? Are there any rival companies in the same business?**
- **Can you tell me more about the company's DNA and future direction?**
- **Regarding the innovation process: Is it "move fast and break things" or precision and artistry?**
- **What is the company's background in healthcare and how did Zivver come about?**
