# 7 Signs You're Not Ready to Lead (Yet)

**Summary:** Early warning signs that indicate you should develop leadership skills before taking on formal leadership responsibilities.

1. Optimizing for efficiency, not influence — leaders create conditions for others to succeed.
2. Addiction to instant results — leadership is a long-game role.
3. Avoiding silence — leaders are comfortable with reflection and measured responses.
4. Thinking leadership is only confidence — leaders act despite uncertainty.
5. Focusing on problems, not patterns — leaders change systems to prevent recurring problems.
6. Needing rigid structure to be productive — leaders create adaptable systems.
7. Seeing leadership as a reward — leadership is service, not status.
