# Architect Competency Matrix

**Summary:** A framework for evaluating and growing as a Software Architect, evolving from technical depth to systems thinking and business influence.

---

## 1. Technical Depth & Innovation
*From Lead Developer to Architect*

- **Modern Stack Proficiency**: Deep expertise in .NET 8+, Azure (PaaS, FaaS) and Distributed Systems.
- **Pattern Mastery**: Implementation and trade-off analysis of SOLID, DDD, CQRS and Event Sourcing.
- **Decision Records**: Ability to author clear, concise **ADR (Architecture Decision Records)** that document the "why" and rejected alternatives.

## 2. Systems Thinking
*Thinking beyond the single service*

- **Boundary Management**: Defining Domain Boundaries (Bounded Contexts) to minimize coupling.
- **Resilience Design**: Implementing Circuit Breakers, Bulkheads and Retry strategies.
- **Observability**: Designing systems for monitoring via OpenTelemetry, tracing and structured logging.
- **Legacy Modernization**: Applying patterns like **Strangler Fig** to evolve legacy monoliths.

## 3. Leadership & Influence
*Leading through expertise, not authority*

- **Cross-Team Alignment**: Brokering technical agreements between teams (e.g., API contracts, shared libraries).
- **Mentorship**: Constructively guiding colleagues on modern methods and architectural standards.
- **Communication**: Translating complex technical risks into business impact for stakeholders.

## 4. Business Alignment & Impact
*Ensuring technical strategy drives revenue*

- **DORA Metrics**: Driving improvement in Deployment Frequency, Lead Time and System Stability.
- **Cost Management**: Optimizing cloud infrastructure spend (Cloud ROI).
- **Product Mindset**: Proactively suggesting technical features that unlock business growth.

---

## Evaluation Checklist
- [ ] Can explain the trade-off of Event Sourcing vs. CRUD.
- [ ] Has led a major tech stack migration or modernization effort.
- [ ] Can define a multi-service communication strategy (Sync vs Async).
- [ ] Is comfortable presenting architectural vision to a Steering Committee.
