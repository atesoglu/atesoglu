# Career Growth

Career development notes, role expectations, onboarding guides, and long-term growth strategies.

## How the Role Notes Fit Together

Most roles have three complementary documents:

- **Role expectations**: what the role is accountable for and how its impact is expressed.
- **Onboarding guide**: an initial orientation and first actions.
- **Long-term success**: growth over time, failure modes, and possible next steps.

These documents are deliberately kept separate because they answer different questions.
