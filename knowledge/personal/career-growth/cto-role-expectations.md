# CTO: Role Expectations

**Summary:** The technical "Visionary" and "Guardian." Accountable for the long-term technological competitiveness and industry-standing of the company.

---

## 1. Core Accountability
The CTO (Chief Technology Officer) ensures that the company's technology is a strategic asset, not a liability. They identify future trends, manage existential technical risks and act as the technical voice to the Board and the Market.

## 2. Impact Areas

### 💡 Vision & Innovation (Primary Focus)
- **Technology Roadmap**: Defines where the technical stack must be in 3-5 years.
- **Innovation Strategy**: Leads R&D efforts and decides when to adopt disruptive technologies.
- **Buy/Build/Partner**: Makes the high-level strategic decisions on core platform dependencies.

### 🛡️ Risk & Governance
- **Cybersecurity & Compliance**: Ultimate accountability for the company's data integrity and security posture.
- **BCP & Disaster Recovery**: Ensures the company can survive catastrophic technical failure.
- **Intellectual Property**: Manages patents and technical proprietary assets (The "Moat").

### 🤝 Market & Investor Relations
- **Board Technical Advisor**: Communicates technical risk and strategy to investors and the Board of Directors.
- **Customer Evangelism**: Partners with Sales on "Visions" for the largest, most strategic customers.
- **Public Brand**: Represents the company in the broader technology ecosystem.
