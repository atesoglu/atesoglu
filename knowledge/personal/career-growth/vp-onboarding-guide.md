# VP of Engineering: The First 30 Days

**Summary:** Transitioning from "Unit Leader" to "Functional Executive."

---

## 1. Days 1–10: The Functional & Market View
- [ ] **C-Suite & Board Intro**: Understand the company's financial health, investor expectations and the 2-3 year market strategy.
- [ ] **Functional Audit**: Evaluate the overall "Engineering engine." How much do we spend on R&D vs. Maintenance? What is our ratio of ICs to Managers?
- [ ] **Market Perception**: Meet with Marketing and Sales Leaders. How is our "product quality" perceived in the market?

## 2. Days 11–20: Identifying Functional Leaks
- [ ] **Talent Density Audit**: Review the 9-box grid for the entire engineering organization. Where are our single-points-of-failure in leadership?
- [ ] **Global Operational Risks**: Review the security posture and massive architectural risks (e.g., data center exit plans).
- [ ] **Policy & Process Review**: Are our global policies (Remote work, Comp bands, Promotions) attracting or repelling top talent?

## 3. Days 21–30: The VP-Level Charter
- [ ] **Define the "Engineering Identity"**: Who are we as an engineering team? (e.g., "We are a speed-focused team" vs "We are a reliability-focused team").
- [ ] **Establish Executive "Pulsing"**: Create the dashboard or reporting structure you will use to communicate engineering health to the Board.
- [ ] **The Functional Priority**: Define the top 3 goals for the *entire* engineering organization for the next year.

---

## 4. Key Behavioral Shift
- **Influence over Action**: You don't manage people; you manage *the system of management*.
- **The Customer is the Business**: Your success is measured by the company's success (Revenue, Growth, Margin), not by sprint points.
