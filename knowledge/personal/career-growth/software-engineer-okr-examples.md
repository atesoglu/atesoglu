# Software Engineer OKR and Goal Examples

**Summary:** Example OKRs to help software engineers structure goals around impact, skills and cross-team collaboration.

**Maximize performance as a Software Engineer with effective goals.**

Check out these examples below to set your own goals.

Including contributions from 1:1s, Managing Up and more.

---

## 1. Improve your network outside of your team

- Attend at least two tech meetups and share learnings with your team to expand knowledge and connections outside of your immediate team.
- Pair programming with at least three engineers outside your immediate team to understand different coding styles and techniques.
- If I have bandwidth in a sprint, offer my help to another team to gain insight into their processes.
- Join an open-source community focused on something new and mentor junior developers within that group.
